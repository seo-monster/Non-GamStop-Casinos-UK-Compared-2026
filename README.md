## How to test a casino before you trust it

1. **Deposit the minimum and withdraw it immediately.** Do not play. What happens next tells you
   more than any review: how long verification takes, what documents are asked for, whether support
   answers.
2. **Upload documents on day one,** not on the day you want your money. ID and a recent utility bill,
   both matching the name on the payment method.
3. **Do not change payment method between deposit and withdrawal.** It restarts the checks nearly
   everywhere.
4. **Read the per-request withdrawal cap and the minimum** before the bonus terms. They decide how a
   win actually reaches you.
5. **Screenshot every withdrawal request.** It is the only record you hold if support later says no
   request exists.
6. **Skip the bonus on the first deposit** if your plan is to withdraw. 400% at 30x with a £0.10
   stake cap is a very long session, not free money.
